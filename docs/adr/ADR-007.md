ADR-007: The Comparer Detects, Does Not Resolve

Status: accepted

Context: The old Resolver mixed two responsibilities (detect candidates / apply merge), hence its instability across versions.

Decision: The Comparer component on Day 2 has only this contract: detect pairs of entities from different sources bearing compatible values, and produce SAME_AS propositions (persistence in DB, application by default per ADR-005). It never merges (ADR-002), creates no other relationship types, and invents no rules. Comparers are interchangeable strategies (exact email match, tomorrow: normalized phones, string distances); composition of multiple contradictory strategies falls under an arbitration policy (open question v0.2, same shape as the "majority policy" for typing).

Consequences: The component is testable in isolation, extensible without touching the pipeline, and its contract fits in one sentence.
