ADR-004: Provenance Persisted on Each Entity

Status: accepted

Context: Co-presence ("these entities were born from the same line") and origin ("this entity came from CSV, not JSON") are essential: for Pre-Ruler (co-presence rules), Comparer (inter-source comparison only), and the auditability required by ADR-002. Yet this information is transient in the pipeline (in-memory groups) and dies upon insertion into the database.

Decision: Each entity persists its provenance: at minimum the source file, ideally also a line/reference record. The Validator produces groups (list[list[Entity]]) preserving the original line during ingestion; lightweight provenance is written to DB on each entity.

Consequences: entities schema extended (source/line column). Comparer can filter on "different sources". In-memory grouping and DB-provenance cover Day 1 and Day 2 respectively.
