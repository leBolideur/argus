ADR-002: No Physical Merging — Identity Is a Relationship (SAME_AS)

Status: accepted

Context: The v1 Resolver detected duplicates without acting ("bastard state"). Physically merging entities would destroy provenance (which source asserted what?), make merges irreversible, and contradict the founding principle "relationships are first-class objects".

Decision: Argus never modifies or deletes source entities. When two entities are deemed identical (detected by Comparer, validated or by default), a SAME_AS relationship is created between them. "Merging" becomes an edge in the graph, not a destructive operation.

Consequences:
Proposition is reversible: deleting the relationship suffices to undo the merge.
Auditability is guaranteed: each datum keeps its source.
The Query Engine (navigation) must traverse SAME_AS chains — known open question: does A SAME_AS B and B SAME_AS C imply A SAME_AS C? (transitivity, to handle in v0.2).
