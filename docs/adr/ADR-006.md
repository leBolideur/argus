ADR-006: Relationship Vocabulary Policy — Three Cases

Status: accepted

Context: The nature of a link (HAS_EMAIL? REPORTS_TO?) cannot always be inferred; proof by "boss_mail".

Decision for v0.1.0:

Seeded vocabulary: relationship types in the starting ontology (HAS_EMAIL, WORKS_FOR, SAME_AS...) are written by the operator once, in DB.
Generic fallback: any co-present pair that matches no rule receives a generic relationship (named LINKED_TO) rather than silence. No pair lost, none over-interpreted.
Naming heuristics (e.g., column name "mail" → propose HAS_EMAIL): deferred to v0.2, with guardrail "boss_mail".
Consequences: The v0.1.0 graph is "honest": what's typed comes from human seeding, the rest is explicitly generic.
